# Plan 13-02: Codebase Concerns & Integrations Remediation

**Phase:** Phase 13 - REACH 2026 Poster & Codebase Concerns Remediation  
**Wave:** 2 of 2 - P0/P1 (Security, Privacy, and Platform Hardening)

---

## 1. Objective

Systematically fix the high-priority architectural concerns and integration gaps documented in `.planning/codebase/CONCERNS.md` and `INTEGRATIONS.md`:
1. **PII Second Layer & Classification Normalization:**
   - Expand `PATTERNS` in `backend/app/services/pii.py` to recognize clinical subject IDs, screening numbers, patient names, and street address indicators.
   - Update `classify_payload` to recognize all 8 active connectors (`pubmed`, `clinical_trials`, `fda`, `ema`, `newsapi`, `biopharmadive`, `fierce_pharma`, `et_pharma`).
   - Default untrusted or unrecognized sources with unscrubbed content to `UNKNOWN` / quarantined.
2. **Production Startup Guard:**
   - In `backend/app/core/config.py`, add `validate_production_settings()` to fail fast if `APP_ENV=production` and `SECRET_KEY == "dev-secret-change-in-production"` or `DEMO_MODE=true`.
3. **NewsAPI UTC Clock Robustness:**
   - In `backend/app/connectors/newsapi.py`, use explicit `datetime.now(timezone.utc).date().isoformat()` for all quota tracking, date window boundaries, and cursor writes.
4. **Scheduler Concurrency & RSS Explicit Intervals:**
   - In `backend/app/core/config.py` and `backend/app/services/scheduler.py`, add explicit settings for trade-press RSS intervals (`SCHEDULER_BIOPHARMA_DIVE_INTERVAL_MINUTES`, `SCHEDULER_FIERCE_PHARMA_INTERVAL_MINUTES`, `SCHEDULER_ET_PHARMA_INTERVAL_MINUTES`).
5. **Automated Verification:**
   - Add automated test suite `tests/test_codebase_concerns.py` to assert PII clinical identifier detection, production boot refusal, UTC date formatting, and WORM audit-trigger immutability.
