# Plan 13-01: REACH 2026 Poster Generation (Multi-Image SVG Synthesis & PNG Conversion)

**Phase:** Phase 13 - REACH 2026 Poster & Codebase Concerns Remediation  
**Wave:** 1 of 2 - P0 (Visual Assets & Oral Presentation Synthesis)

---

## 1. Objective

Create an award-winning, competition-ready scientific poster conforming to all guidelines in `poster/`:
1. **Dimensions & Orientation:** 90 cm width × 120 cm height (standard 3:4 portrait, 3402 × 4536 pixels).
2. **Single SVG Combining Multiple Photos/Diagrams:**
   - Architecture Diagram (`pitch/pngs/architecture.png`)
   - 10-Node Intelligence Dataflow (`pitch/pngs/dataflow.png`)
   - Cross-Functional Responsibility Matrix (`pitch/pngs/responsibility_flow.png`)
   - MetaRadar Logo (`pitch/pngs/icon.png`)
   - ProClin Research Private Limited Logo (`poster/extracted_assets/REACH_2026_Conc_p1_1.png`)
   - REACH 2026 Conclave Emblem (`poster/extracted_assets/REACH_2026_Conc_p1_0.png`)
3. **Conference Constraints:**
   - Theme: *"Generative AI & Decentralized Clinical Trials: Transforming Clinical Research"*
   - Upper section visual prominence (Title, Academic Affiliation with MSRIT / Dr. B V Basavaraj, Abstract, Architecture).
   - Entire poster in English with high-contrast palette and large readable typography.
   - Structured abstract under 250 words.
   - 5-minute presentation oral script.
4. **Automated Generation & Rasterization:**
   - Python generator `poster/generate_poster.py` that emits `poster/poster.svg`.
   - Headless Chrome rendering command that rasterizes `poster.svg` to high-resolution `poster/poster.png`.
   - Companion `poster/ABSTRACT.md` containing the abstract and 5-minute presentation script.

## 2. Deliverables & Files Created

### A. [NEW] `poster/generate_poster.py`
- Reads visual diagrams and logos, encodes them as base64 data URIs.
- Builds the 3402 × 4536 SVG canvas with:
  - Header: ProClin & REACH 2026 badges, MetaRadar icon, Conference Theme banner, title, authors, affiliations.
  - Top Zone: Structured Abstract card (<250 words) + Clinical Trial Challenges card.
  - Middle Zone: Multi-Agent Architecture visual (Local Gemma 3 4B + Grok privacy gate) + 10-Node LangGraph Pipeline visual.
  - Bottom Zone: Cross-Functional Governance visual (6 stakeholder queues) + Deterministic 4-factor scoring + Validation & ICH-GCP E6(R3) compliance matrix.
- Executes headless Chrome command to render `poster/poster.png`.

### B. [NEW] `poster/poster.svg`
- Self-contained master SVG poster file with all embedded graphics, vector badges, and typography.

### C. [NEW] `poster/poster.png`
- Print-ready 3402 × 4536 PNG rasterized from `poster.svg`.

### D. [NEW] `poster/ABSTRACT.md`
- Contains:
  1. Title & Authors/Collaborators.
  2. Structured Abstract (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion - 245 words).
  3. 5-Minute Oral Presentation Pitch Script timed to 300 seconds.
